Privacy Policy

Daily Installment Reminder 1

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Data Collection
We collect only the data you provide: customer names, phone numbers, installment details, and your staff profile information. No data is collected automatically.
2. Data Storage
All data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not store or transmit any data to external servers.
3. SMS Sending
SMS messages are sent directly from your phone's SIM using the Web SMS API when available. We do not intercept or store the content of messages you send.
4. Staff Data Transfer
When you export or import profiles, the data remains under your full control. Imported profiles are kept completely separate and never mixed with existing data.
5. No Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third parties.
6. Your Control
You can delete individual customers, entire centers, or entire profiles at any time. You can also export or reset all data from within the app.
7. Contact
For any privacy-related questions, please contact the app owner.
